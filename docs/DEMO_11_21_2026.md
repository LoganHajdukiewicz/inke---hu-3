## What made the earlier demos work (and what's carried over)

**Feb 24 2026 demo** (`test/movement_demo_02_24_2026.tscn`, the reference):
- **S-1GN the sign is the host.** 18 signs plus 2 surprise world triggers
  hold the whole tutorial, in his voice: needy, lying, kaomoji, "But you're so
  smart I bet you already knew that." He teaches by trolling ("This floor
  falls!!!" after you're already on it).
- **One idea per beat**, each taught in safety and then tested:
  move/dash/run → chasm jump → first enemy → paint/gears explained →
  heal paint → rail grind → spring floor → high ledge → grapple → falling
  floors → wall jump corridor → spinning floors → ice over lava → save
  paint → grapple chain → rail cascade → arena with 15 inklings → CRED → win.
- **Checkpoints every ~1–2 minutes**, with a long final arena and a CRED payoff.
- Everything is **big and readable**: 20m-wide floors, sightlines ahead.

**Jul 24 2025 demo**: S-1GN's backstory (Bunbun the bunny, his sanctuary),
the HU3 intro joke, and the merchant intro.

**Open-world draft (Sep 2026)**: quest givers with ! marks, a CRED gate, and a
Sludge King boss. Its problems were scale (a 500m causeway, mostly empty
travel), no host personality, and quests that didn't teach anything.

## The new demo: a small island, quests unlock a boss

Feb 24's feel (S-1GN hosting, one-idea beats, big readable spaces), but
**open**: a ~240m island with a hub town and three spokes. Each spoke is a
short themed platforming gauntlet built from the Feb 24 beats and ends at a
quest goal. Three completed quests = enough CRED for the boss.

```
                 N  ▲  SUMMIT (spoke 2: vertical)
                    │  climb fence · ladder · swing bars · wall-jump chimney
                    │  · grapple chain → Echo's flag
   W  SHIPWRECK     │
   (spoke 1: floors)├──── HUB TOWN (spawn) ──── E  JUNKYARD (spoke 3: combat)
   spring · moving ·│     S-1GN, merchant,      rails · inkling camp ·
   falling · spin · │     3 quest givers,       turret · charger ·
   ice-over-sludge  │     boss gate (CRED 45)   Moxie's crates
                    │
                 S  ▼  BEACH → ARENA ISLET (boss) via spring + rail
```

### Run plan (≈10 min)

| Time | Beat | Teaches |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Spawn on the beach, S-1GN #1: "you stopped to read me" + controls | move, run, camera, dash |
| 0:45 | Hub: HU3/S-1GN banter, merchant (double jump + wall jump on sale), 3 quest givers with ! | quests, gears, CRED |
| 1:30 | **Spoke 1 – Shipwreck (Rust):** "my gear crate sank" → spring, moving and falling floors, spinning deck, ice over sludge → 6 gears | floor types |
| 3:30 | **Spoke 2 – Summit (Echo):** reach the flag → fence climb, ladder, swing bars, wall-jump chimney, grapple chain, ledge shimmy | traversal |
| 5:30 | **Spoke 3 – Junkyard (Moxie):** smash 6 crates in an inkling camp → rail into the camp, stomp/spin/slam, turret, charger | combat |
| 7:30 | Back at the hub: 45 CRED → gate opens, S-1GN panics | |
| 8:00 | **Boss – Sludge King** on the arena islet (spring + rail to get there): 3 phases, grunts, barrels | everything |
| 9:30 | Win: CRED payout, S-1GN "I guess I am a sign of my word", → You Win | |

Quests can be done in **any order**. Each spoke also has a hidden spray can
and a bonus CRED medallion for explorers.

### CRED math
Quests pay 15 each (45). The boss gate needs 45, so all three quests unlock
it. The bonus medallions (5 each) are pure score.
